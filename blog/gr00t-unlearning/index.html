Forgetting a task in GR00T: first unlearning results

After learning tasks A then B, SalUn (lr 3e-5) takes task B from — to — success while task A stays at —. Gradient ascent and GradDiff wipe out both tasks, and SSD leaves the policy unchanged.

Calibration run, LIBERO-Goal in simulation. Success rates are out of 50 rollouts, shown as a fraction with a 95% Wilson interval.

Fine-tuning learns each task almost perfectly

Zero-shot, the base model solves neither task. After 2k steps on task A it already succeeds in about nine of ten rollouts, and learning B afterwards does not hurt A.

Flow-matching loss on 32 fixed frames per task, measured every 500 steps (log scale, lower means the policy predicts that task's actions better). Training on A raises the loss on every unseen task above the base model's. Training on B then lowers C, D and E again, so the two tasks share structure.

Gradient ascent and GradDiff also reach zero on B, but only because the policy is destroyed: A falls to zero too and the probe loss diverges to between 106 and 1013. NPO and VLA-Forget did not run in this calibration: NPO ran out of memory on a 48 GB GPU, and VLA-Forget hit a batching bug that has been fixed since.

SalUn removes the behaviour but only part of the knowledge

Rollouts on B fail completely after SalUn, but B's probe loss moves only — to — of the way from the model before unlearning to the retrain oracle. The policy may still hold much of what it learned on B. That residue is what the stream benchmark's relapse metric is designed to detect.

Limits of these numbers

  • One stream step only (learn A, learn B, forget B). The full benchmark runs a five-task stream with repeated learn and unlearn operations.
  • Each unlearning variant was evaluated with one seed of 50 rollouts. The learning results use two seeds.
  • The run used the code of Nov 4 on an RTX A6000 machine. The cluster runs (96 GB Blackwell and 48 GB MIG slices) have not finished yet.
  • Hyperparameters are a first grid (three learning rates or three SSD thresholds), not tuned.
Metric definitions
SR (success rate)
Fraction of 50 simulated rollouts that complete the task within 300 control steps.
Gap to oracle
Mean absolute difference in success rate on A and B between the unlearned model and the retrain oracle (LA, which never saw B). Zero is ideal.
Probe forget ratio
(probe loss on B after unlearning − before) ÷ (oracle's probe loss on B − before). 0 means nothing changed and 1 means B's loss matches a model that never learned B.
Probe loss
GR00T's flow-matching training loss on 32 fixed frames of a task, with fixed noise, so values are comparable across checkpoints.

Data

Every number on this page comes from these files, computed from the calibration run's evaluation and training logs.

  • unlearning.csv: one row per unlearning variant
  • learning_sr.csv: learning success rates per eval seed
  • learning_probe.csv: probe loss per task during LA and LB
  • unlearning_probe.csv: probe loss during SalUn unlearning
  • references.csv: retrain oracle and pre-unlearning model
