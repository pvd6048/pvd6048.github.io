01 / About

Hi, I'm Pranav! I'm an undergraduate at IIT Guwahati studying Electronics & Communication Engineering with a minor in Data Science & AI. I work on robot learning and learning-based control: building systems that let robots learn to act competently and adaptively in the physical world. My recent work combines reinforcement learning with model predictive control for autonomous vehicles, and I'm broadly interested in robust, efficient and adaptive decision-making for embodied agents.
